# BeaconGame - Beacon Attack & Defense

A fast-paced PvP minigame for Minecraft servers. Two teams fight over beacons: Defenders hide and protect them, Attackers must find and destroy them before time runs out.

## How to Play
- Two teams: Attackers and Defenders.
- Defenders place beacons during a setup phase.
- Attackers are blinded and frozen during setup to prevent peeking.
- Once the attack phase begins, PvP turns on.
- Attackers use a tracking compass to locate beacons.
- Destroy all beacons to win as Attacker. Survive until time runs out to win as Defender.

## Skill System
Choose one of three paths at the start:
- **Assault** - charge, break shields, deal heavy damage.
- **Disruption** - blind, slow, and hide from enemies.
- **Support** - supply teammates, counter defenses, restore the battlefield.

Earn kills to level up your skills during the match.

## Events
Random events shake up the battle: airdrops, buffs/debuffs, nuclear strikes, position swaps, skill upgrades, and a final assault.

## Features
- Multiple arenas to choose from.
- Deaths are safe: no item loss, instant respawn.
- Maps automatically restore after each match.
- Leaderboards track wins, kills, and beacon breaks.

## Commands
- `/bg join [map]` - join a match.
- `/bg leave` - leave the match.
- `/bg arenas` - list available arenas.
- `/bg top` - view leaderboards.
- `/bg skill info` - check your skill progress.
- `/hub` - return to lobby.

# BeaconGame - 信标攻防

一个快节奏的 Minecraft PvP 小游戏。两队围绕信标展开攻防：防守方埋藏并保护信标，进攻方必须在时间结束前找到并摧毁它们。

## 玩法
- 分为进攻方和防守方两队。
- 防守方在准备阶段放置信标。
- 准备阶段进攻方被致盲并冻结，防止偷看。
- 进攻阶段开始后，PvP 开启。
- 进攻方使用追踪指南针定位信标。
- 进攻方摧毁全部信标获胜；防守方坚持到时间结束获胜。

## 技能系统
开局选择一条路线：
- **突袭** - 冲锋、破盾、高爆发伤害。
- **干扰** - 致盲、减速、隐身。
- **辅助** - 补给队友、反制工事、恢复战场。

击杀敌人可升级技能。

## 事件
随机事件搅动战局：空投、增益/减益、核打击、移形换影、技能强化、终局总攻。

## 特色
- 多张竞技场可选。
- 死亡不掉落，秒回重生点。
- 每局结束后地图自动还原。
- 排行榜记录胜场、击杀和破标数。

## 命令
- `/bg join [地图]` - 加入对局。
- `/bg leave` - 离开对局。
- `/bg arenas` - 查看可用地图。
- `/bg top` - 查看排行榜。
- `/bg skill info` - 查看技能进度。
- `/hub` - 返回大厅。
